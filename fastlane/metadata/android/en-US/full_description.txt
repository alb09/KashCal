The family calendar lives in iCloud, work lives in Google, and your Nextcloud hums away in a closet next to the router. KashCal shows all of it in one app.

It has no ads, needs no account, and has no idea what you did last summer!

Type "Coffee with Kash tomorrow 3pm" and it's on the calendar. Plan the week on one page, print it for the fridge, and send your free times instead of playing email ping-pong. Add the school terms and bin day once, and stop asking the group chat.

Open source, in 63 languages, and brought to you by folks who also forget birthdays. Now it remembers them, so you just buy the gift.

https://kashcal.onekash.org
